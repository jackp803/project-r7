# Product entry observation v0.2

Profile: `product-entry-observation-v0.2`. E5 observation interpretation only;
no effect, financial consent, plan renewal or provider capability is issued.

The existing `build_entry_projection` remains strict: its canonical request
quantity must equal the E5 ApprovedTradePlan quantity, and its PAPER position
identity remains unchanged. No legacy profile or test is relabelled.

The additive `build_product_entry_projection` accepts a positive actual canonical
E4 request quantity at or below the original approved exposure upper bound. Every
other request safety field and deterministic internal identity must match the
actual existing E5 gateway. E4 owns native contract conversion and quantization;
E5 receives no native size, metadata, provider symbol or conversion calculation.

Both entry points share the existing E5 fill/order/net-position reconciliation,
first-fill anchor, lifecycle builders and FP12 execution-binding producers. An
ACK cannot create exposure. Actual unique fills, exact accumulated quantity and
average price, and a consistent canonical net-position observation are required.
The original plan stays immutable. A product position uses the deterministic
`r7pos_` identity prefix; it cannot reinterpret a PAPER position as a product
position. Shared Position/FP12 profiles remain unchanged.

Current E6 approval, selected E5 policy, actual E7 process and issuer-owned E4
preparation are still independently required immediately before each effect.
This pure interpretation does not verify a real provider account or real trading.
