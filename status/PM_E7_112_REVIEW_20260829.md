## Required remediation — external consumer participation

Accepted contract `runtime-preflight-v0.1` states:

- `external_consumer_evidence` may be `null` **only when the declared role proves no external orchestrator materially participates**;
- when an external orchestrator, launcher, AgentBridge supervisor or operator-owned runtime materially participates, exact external-consumer compatibility evidence is required.

Worker handoff also states that conditional external participation fails closed when supplied supervisor/**external authority** demonstrates participation.

Current implementation computes:

```text
external_required = role in {SHADOW_RUNTIME, BOUNDED_LIVE_FIRE_RUNTIME} OR supervisor_present
```

but does not include the supplied `RuntimePreflightAuthority.external_consumer_authority` participation fact.

Therefore a `CREDENTIAL_FREE_LOCAL_VERIFICATION` or `PROVIDER_READ_ONLY_OBSERVATION` input can omit `external_consumer_evidence`, set `supervisor_present=false`, while caller-supplied `external_consumer_authority` is non-null/current, and the evaluator can fail to emit `PREFLIGHT_EXTERNAL_CONSUMER_NOT_ACCEPTED`. That can create false `ELIGIBLE` evidence despite current authority demonstrating material external participation.

This is fail-open relative to the accepted profile and must be corrected before merge.

## Remediation requirement

A fresh E7 task must, without changing the shared contract:

1. treat non-null/current supplied external-consumer authority as material participation requiring exact `external_consumer_evidence`, in addition to fixed-role and supervisor-triggered requirements;
2. fail closed with accepted `PREFLIGHT_EXTERNAL_CONSUMER_NOT_ACCEPTED` when participation is required but evidence is missing, incompatible, stale/mismatched, or authority/evidence disagree;
3. preserve SHADOW and bounded-live-fire unconditional external-consumer requirements;
4. preserve PAPER conditional requirement when external supervisor/operator participates;
5. preserve credential-free/provider-read-only conditional requirement when external runner/operator participates;
6. add deterministic regression definitions for at least credential-free and provider-read-only cases where `external_consumer_authority` is present but `external_consumer_evidence` is absent;
7. verify the no-external case remains eligible only when the current authority also represents no material external consumer;
8. keep all provider/runtime/capital side effects absent.

Do not weaken the contract, invent external participation from historical evidence, or modify AgentBridge/operator infrastructure.
